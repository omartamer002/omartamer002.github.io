My journey began in the vibrant heart of Cairo, Egypt, where an early obsession with how things work led me to pursue a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science. As I mastered the principles of software design and distributed systems, I realized a fundamental truth: a system is only as powerful as it is secure.

At 23, I operate at the vital boundary where software engineering meets defensive security. By day, I design clean, high-performance web applications; by night, I work in the trenches as a Cybersecurity SOC Analyst, monitoring networks, hunting threats, and safeguarding critical digital assets.

My ultimate ambition is to pioneer the perfect intersection of code and security pioneering architectures that are resilient by default, and engineering solutions that are secure by design from the very first line of code.
